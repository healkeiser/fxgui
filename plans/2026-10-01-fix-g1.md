## Findings

### _breadcrumb.py

- D1 done. A segment paint reads `fxstyle.colors()`. The strip colours
  come from a cache keyed on the colours themselves (`_worked_out`), so
  `readable_ink` runs again only when a colour changes.
- D2 done. Back and forward share one `_step`, and emit a copy.
- B1 done. A home click emits `home_clicked` only. It goes to
  `home_path()`, or to the first segment.
- D3 done. `_update_nav_buttons` holds the one `show_navigation` check.
- S1 done:
  - `path()` is a method now.
  - `home_path()` and `set_home_path()` replace the property.
  - `RADIUS` is gone; `fxstyle.BUTTON_RADIUS` is used instead.
- The 6.5.3 lambda is now `lambda _=False, idx=index: ...`.
- X3 not done (flat button styled per widget). A segment paints its own
  accent tint under the pointer. `fxRole="flat"` fills `@state_hover` on
  hover, which would cover that tint. On light themes it would also
  match the strip's resting fill. So the segment keeps its own small
  shape rule.

### _collapsible.py

- D1 done. `_is_expanded` is the one expanded state:

  - `_on_toggle_clicked` is gone.

  open. The docstring now gives the real default (300; 0 means no

- D2 done. These are methods now: `title()`/`set_title()`,

  tests now read the animation's `valueChanged`.
- X8 done. The `icon_name` property is gone. `NO_CAP` stays the one

### _accordion.py

- `if content:` is now `is not None`.

### _tag_input.py

- B1 done. Home, Left, Space, Right and End work when the timeline has
  focus. I and O work too when the in/out controls are shown. A click on

- B2 done:

## One thing I'm not sure about

While I rewrote the timeline, the whole pytest process crashed (access
violation) after a test that showed a timeline and dropped its last
Python reference.

- It went away when the transport buttons were made with no parent
  (the layout parents them), as the old code did.
- Making the buttons with a parent but no icon also made it go away.
- A plain QWidget with one parented, icon-bearing QPushButton does not
  crash.

So I do not know the root cause. The workaround is commented at
`_button` in _timeline_slider.py. If another widget builds buttons with
`QPushButton(self)` plus `fxicons.set_icon`, it may be worth a look.
