## Tooltip fix (c2253941)

A shown `FXTooltip` now hides on its anchor's Hide event, so it goes away
when the anchor or the anchor's window closes. The access violation did not
come from hiding on Hide. It came from the anchor's `destroyed` signal,
which was connected straight to a tooltip method: dropping the tooltip
could free the anchor, and the signal then called into the half-deleted
tooltip. The signal now goes through a small function holding only a weak
reference to the tooltip. Test: `tests/test_tooltip_anchor_hidden.py`.

ls-pipeline: nothing to change; any workaround that hides tooltips on close
can go.
