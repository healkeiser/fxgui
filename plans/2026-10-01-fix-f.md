## What the three widgets are now

One mechanism: `QLineEdit.addAction(icon, position)`, written once in
FXIconLineEdit. The other two subclass it.

| Widget | Is | Icon | Gone |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FXIconLineEdit(parent=None, icon_name=None, icon_position="left")` | a QLineEdit | one action, leading or trailing | `.icon_button`, the hand-moved QPushButton, the 5/18/22 px numbers, `resizeEvent`, its QSS |
| `FXPasswordLineEdit(parent=None, icon_position="right")` | the QLineEdit itself | one checkable "visibility" action | `.line_edit`, `.reveal_button`, `toggle_reveal()`, the QHBoxLayout wrapper, its QSS |
| `FXSearchBar(parent=None, placeholder="Search...", debounce_ms=300)` | the QLineEdit itself | leading "search" action + `setClearButtonEnabled(True)` | the container, both QPushButtons, the eventFilter + `focused` property + repolish, `line_edit()`, the `text` property, `set_placeholder()`, the filter dropdown (`show_filter`, `filters`, `set_filters`, `show_filter()`, `filter`, `filter_changed`), all its QSS |

The clear button's icon is the theme's "close": FXProxyStyle already maps
`SP_LineEditClearButton` (fxstyle.py:1605). Inside a host without the proxy
style it is the host's own clear icon.
